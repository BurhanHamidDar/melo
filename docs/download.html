About this app

🎤 Word-by-Word Lyrics

Synced karaoke-style lyrics pulled from multiple global providers, perfectly synchronized with playback and video streams.

🎛️ Audio Lab & Equalizer

10-band precision equalizer, customizable sound profiles, bass boost, spatializer, and smooth crossfade between tracks.

☁️ Instant Cloud Sync

Real-time encrypted sync of your playlists, favorites, and history across all your devices powered by modern cloud infrastructure.

💎 Liquid Glass Styling

AMOLED dark mode, dynamic album-matching backdrop gradients, customizable player widgets, and modern Material 3 fluid physics.

📶 Smart Data Saver

Automatic stream quality adaptation when on cellular data, caching audio and album art locally to save bandwidth.

🛡️ 100% Ad-Free & Private

No audio ads, no tracking beacons, and no paywalls. Built for music lovers who appreciate clean, high-fidelity sound.
